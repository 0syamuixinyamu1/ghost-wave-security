# Limitations

- The service graph is synthetic and fixed.
- Collapse Index weights are heuristic.
- The E8-to-configuration mapping is abstract.
- The sheaf component is finite and minimal.
- Core breach is a stochastic hazard, not a mechanistic privilege-escalation model.
- The attacker is adaptive but highly simplified.
- Operational cost units are synthetic, including the explicit core-breach penalty.
- No conclusion should be transferred to hospitals, banks, public infrastructure,
  industrial-control systems, or production cloud environments.

The correct interpretation is comparative:

> Under the same toy dynamics, how do recovery policies behave when candidate
> diversity, attacker-state observability, and exposure cost are controlled?
